### Added

- First public release: *Waiting on you*, *No reply yet*, *Running*, *Background jobs*, *Scheduled jobs*, *Recently finished* and *Standing goals* sections.
- **Open in Discord** buttons that jump to a thread in the Discord desktop app, landing on your message when its ID is known.
- English, Simplified Chinese, Traditional Chinese and Japanese UI, following the dashboard's language setting.
- Optional `settings.json`: owner, privacy and tunable windows.
- Per-section degradation with a warning banner when Hermes's internal schema changes.
